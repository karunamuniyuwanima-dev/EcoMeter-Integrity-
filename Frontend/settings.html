Frequently Asked Questions
What is False Data Injection Attack (FDIA)? +

FDIA is a cyberattack where an attacker manipulates sensor readings or meter data to deceive the energy management system. EcoMeter detects these by comparing readings against statistical baselines using Z-score and IQR methods.

How does the anomaly detection work? +

The system analyzes each meter reading against a statistical baseline (mean, standard deviation, IQR) computed from historical data. Readings that deviate beyond defined thresholds are flagged as anomalies and assigned a risk score from 0–100.

What do the risk levels mean? +

Critical (75–100) — Severe anomaly, immediate action needed.
High (50–74) — Significant deviation, investigation recommended.
Medium (25–49) — Moderate anomaly, monitor closely.
Low (0–24) — Minor deviation, likely normal variation.

How do I export reports? +

Go to the Reports page, apply your desired filters (date range, meter ID), then scroll down to the data table and click the Export CSV button. The file will download automatically.

What meters are monitored? +

EcoMeter monitors 5 smart meters (M001–M005). Each meter records energy consumption (kWh), voltage, current, power factor, and temperature at 15-minute intervals. All readings are stored in the Supabase cloud database.

How do I reset my password? +

On the login page click Forgot Password?, enter your registered email address, and an OTP (One-Time Password) will be sent to your email. Enter the OTP to verify and set a new password.
